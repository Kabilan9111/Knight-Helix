# 🚀 Key Features

SANCHALAN brings together ten core capabilities designed to provide a deeper understanding of real-world project execution.

---

<h3><font color=#DC2626>01 · Intelligent Multi-Source Data Capture</font></h3>

SANCHALAN captures execution information from **DPRs, Excel files, PDFs, text reports, voice updates, images and site reports**.  
OCR, ASR, computer vision and LLM-based extraction convert unstructured field information into structured execution events.

---

<h3><font color="#DC2626">02 · L5/L6 Activity Extraction & Semantic Linking</font></h3>

The platform identifies the **correct planned L5/L6 activity** associated with an incoming field report, even when the wording differs from the schedule.  
Semantic similarity, fuzzy matching, location, discipline, identifiers, dates and contextual metadata are used to rank candidate activities and generate a confidence score.

---

<h3><font color="#DC2626">03 · Geo-Temporal & Environmental Evidence Verification</font></h3>

SANCHALAN evaluates field evidence using **GPS, geofencing, recent location presence, timestamps, images and environmental or weather context**.  
These signals are combined into an evidence-confidence assessment that can distinguish between verified, partially verified, location-unverified and review-required submissions.

---

<h3><font color="#DC2626">04 · Live Project Execution Graph</font></h3>

Project activities are represented as **connected execution nodes**, while schedule dependencies form the relationships between them.  
Each activity can maintain planned and actual dates, progress, status, team, evidence, confidence, dependencies and risk, providing a continuously updated representation of the project's execution state.

---

<h3><font color="#DC2626">05 · Plan–Reality Conflict Intelligence</font></h3>

SANCHALAN compares the **baseline project plan and dependency structure** against observed field execution.  
It identifies sequence, timing and dependency deviations as reviewable conflicts, allowing project teams to determine whether actual execution is still aligned with the original plan.

---

<h3><font color="#DC2626">06 · Delay Ripple Engine</font></h3>

A delay in one activity can expose multiple downstream activities to schedule risk.  
The Delay Ripple Engine traverses genuine dependency relationships and evaluates factors such as float, progress, remaining duration, dependency type and critical-path information to identify potential downstream impact without falsely marking every affected activity as delayed.

---

<h3><font color="#DC2626">07 · Cross-Project Institutional Memory</font></h3>

SANCHALAN retains valuable execution knowledge from previous projects, including **durations, delays, causes, weather, terrain, resources, productivity, outcomes and mitigation actions**.  
Historical patterns can then be retrieved when similar conditions appear in a current project, providing evidence-based decision support rather than blindly assuming historical outcomes will repeat.

---

<h3><font color="#DC2626">08 · Proactive Agentic Decision Loop</font></h3>

The platform combines the **current execution state, risk propagation, historical memory, resources, dependencies and environmental conditions** to generate potential recovery recommendations.  
Critical actions remain under **human approval**, following a controlled approach where AI detects, reasons and recommends while authorized users approve and execute important interventions.

---

<h3><font color="#DC2626">09 · AI-Powered Execution Visualization</font></h3>

SANCHALAN transforms real project execution data into an intuitive visual intelligence layer showing **progress, status, dependencies, risk, verification state, bottlenecks and downstream impact**.  
Visual indicators are derived from the application's actual execution state and deterministic business rules rather than fabricated or randomly generated project information.

---

<h3><font color="#DC2626">10 · Live Spatial Verification & GPS Path Tracing</font></h3>

During site verification, the Site Engineer can use the device's **real browser geolocation capabilities** to capture live latitude, longitude, accuracy and timestamps.  
The system follows the engineer on the map, records accepted GPS points, calculates actual walked distance and renders the captured route as a **glowing golden path**, preserving the spatial trace as verification evidence.

## 🎯 What SANCHALAN Enables

### From Fragmented Reports to Execution Intelligence

SANCHALAN is designed to help project teams understand not only individual task updates, but the broader state of project execution.

It provides visibility into:

- **What is happening now**
- **What differs from the original plan**
- **Which activities are at risk**
- **How delays may propagate**
- **What historical projects can teach us**
- **What evidence supports an execution update**
- **Where field verification actually occurred**
- **What corrective actions could be considered**

---

## 🧠 Evidence-Backed Project Intelligence

SANCHALAN is built around a simple principle:

> **Project intelligence should be grounded in project evidence.**

Execution status, risk, verification, dependency impact and recommendations should be traceable to the underlying project data and supporting evidence.

The platform avoids false certainty when information is incomplete, GPS accuracy is poor, activity matching is uncertain, dependencies are missing or historical evidence is insufficient.
